# ACF-velden Van Ginkel Houtbouw

De ACF-velden worden automatisch geregistreerd via:

```text
wp-content/themes/vanginkelhoutbouw/inc/acf-fields.php
```

Je hoeft deze velden dus niet handmatig in ACF te koppelen.

## Templates

### Home
Template: `page-templates/template-home.php`

Velden:
- hero_kicker
- hero_title
- hero_text
- hero_primary_label
- hero_primary_url
- hero_secondary_label
- hero_secondary_url
- hero_3d_enabled
- intro_title
- intro_text
- services_title
- services_intro
- services_items
- process_title
- process_items
- cta_title
- cta_text
- cta_button_label
- cta_button_url

### Contact
Template: `page-templates/template-contact.php`

Velden:
- contact_intro_title
- contact_intro_text
- contact_phone
- contact_email
- contact_address
- contact_form_shortcode

### Configurator
Template: `page-templates/template-configurator.php`

Velden:
- configurator_title
- configurator_intro
